What Ternah for Factories changes for manufacturers

Production batches follow their formulas, and missing materials are flagged before a batch starts instead of halfway through. Stock moves between the factory and its branches with approvals, and cashiers, debtors and staff sit in the same system, so the owner sees production and sales in one place.
