Pearl Educate

A learning app for children, built on the learning science principles of Carnegie Mellon University's Learning Sciences for Innovators programme. Every ten-minute lesson is designed for memory formation: children practise recalling answers with no score, get a hint when they are stuck, and each question comes back after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days so what they learn stays with them.

  • Built on CMU learning science principles for memory formation

SACCOs and microfinance

Ternah Core Banking

Core banking for SACCOs and microfinance institutions. It runs member accounts, savings, fixed deposits, teller deposits and withdrawals, and every loan from application to repayment. CRB checks are built into loan assessment, so officers see a borrower’s credit history before they approve. Members get an SMS for every deposit, withdrawal and repayment due, and can check balances and statements by USSD from any phone, with no smartphone or data needed.

  • Core banking: members, savings, fixed deposits and tellers
  • CRB checks built into loan assessment and approval
  • SMS alerts for every transaction and repayment due
  • USSD code for balances and statements on any phone

Case study · Ternah Health

A private hospital with outpatients, a lab and a pharmacy

The problem
Patients queued at every desk, and lab results travelled to doctors on paper.
What we set up
Reception, doctor, lab and pharmacy on one patient record, with a live queue on each screen.
What changed
Results reach the doctor the moment the lab saves them, and reception can see who is waiting where.

We describe the type of hospital rather than naming it.

Case study · Ternah for Factories

A manufacturer with one factory and several branch shops

The problem
Batches stopped halfway when a material ran out, and stock sent to the branches was tracked in notebooks.
What we set up
Production batches from saved formulas, a check for missing materials before each batch starts, and stock transfers to branches that need approval.
What changed
Batches start only when the materials are there, and the owner sees production, branch stock and sales in one place.

We describe the type of manufacturer rather than naming it.

Case study · Ternah Pharmacy

A pharmacy with more than one branch

The problem
Expired stock turned up on the shelves, and the books were pieced together from receipts at the end of the month.
What we set up
Dispensing and the till in one place, every batch tracked with low-stock and expiry alerts, and sales that keep recording when the internet drops.
What changed
Expiring batches show up before they expire, and each branch’s sales and stock can be compared on any day.

We describe the type of pharmacy rather than naming it.
