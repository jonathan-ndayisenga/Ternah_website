What it runs

Everything a SACCO does, in one system.

From the day a member joins to the last loan repayment.

Members and accounts

Register individuals, groups and companies, and open joint accounts. Each member has one record that holds their savings, loans and statements.

  • Individual, group and company members
  • Joint accounts
  • Active and inactive members at a glance

Savings

Every savings account in one portfolio. The system keeps what a member can withdraw apart from savings that are frozen or held.

  • Total, available and frozen balances
  • Fixed deposits
  • Locked or held funds kept separate

Deposits and withdrawals

Tellers record deposits and withdrawals at the counter, and the member gets an SMS before they leave.

  • Teller deposits and withdrawals
  • An SMS for every transaction
  • Each teller’s totals for the day

Loans

Every loan from application to the last repayment. The borrower’s CRB report sits inside the assessment, so officers see their credit history before they approve.

  • CRB checks built into assessment
  • Approval, disbursement and repayment schedules
  • SMS reminders before a repayment is due

SMS and USSD

Members check balances and statements by USSD from any phone, with no smartphone or data needed. The dashboard shows how many members use it.

  • Balances and statements by USSD
  • SMS alerts for deposits, withdrawals and repayments
  • Mobile banking adoption rate

Dashboard and reports

The manager opens one screen and sees members, loans and savings for the branch, with a report behind each number.

  • Members by gender and status
  • Clients with active loans
  • Savings portfolio and mobile banking

For members

How a member is served.

Most of it happens on their phone, without a trip to the branch.

  1. 1

    Join

    Register as an individual, a group or a company, or open a joint account.

  2. 2

    Save

    Deposit at the teller and get an SMS before leaving the counter.

  3. 3

    Borrow

    Apply for a loan. The officer sees the CRB report and approves.

  4. 4

    Repay

    Get an SMS reminder before each repayment is due.

  5. 5

    Check

    Dial the USSD code for a balance or statement on any phone.
