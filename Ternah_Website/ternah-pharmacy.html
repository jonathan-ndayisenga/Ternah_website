What it does

The whole pharmacy, not just the till.

Each part hands off to the next, so nothing gets typed twice and nothing goes missing between the counter and the books.

From prescription to receipt

The dispenser builds the bill and sends it to the cashier, who takes cash, mobile money, card, insurance, a client account or a prepaid card, and prints an 80mm receipt.

Stock that knows its expiry

Every delivery is a batch with its own cost and expiry date. Low stock and drugs about to expire are flagged before they cost you money.

Refunds need a manager

A cashier can’t hand money back alone. They raise the refund or swap, the branch manager approves it, and stock and books update together.

Books that keep themselves

Every sale, payment, delivery and expense posts to a proper ledger. Cashbook, trial balance, balance sheet and income statement are always up to date.

Suppliers and what you owe

Take deliveries on credit, part-pay as you go, and see every supplier balance, and every client or insurer who owes you, at a glance.

Keeps selling offline

A branch can run on a local hub when the internet goes down. Dispensing and payments carry on, and everything syncs once it’s back.

Who it’s for

Everyone sees what their job needs.

Five roles, each with its own screens. Nobody wades through menus that aren’t theirs, and nobody sees figures they shouldn’t.

Owner

Every branch’s sales, profit, stock and team.

Branch manager

Stock, suppliers, approvals and the branch’s books.

Accountant

The full ledger and reports across all branches.

Dispenser

Builds bills and dispenses and, if allowed, checks other branches’ stock.

Cashier

Takes payment, prints receipts, handles insurance and credit.

On the phone in your pocket

A manager can approve a refund from the floor, and an owner can check today’s sales from anywhere, with no separate app to install.
